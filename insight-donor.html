Donor requirements · Practical explainer

From agreement to everyday obligations.

Different agreements can set different conditions. Start with the signed agreement, relevant annexes and approved amendments, and confirm how these interact with organisational policies.

Make the requirements visible

Record each obligation, its source, the responsible person, the deadline or trigger and the evidence needed. Include reporting dates, cost conditions, procurement provisions, prior approvals and record retention where applicable.

Bring requirements into planning

Discuss obligations with finance, programme, procurement and partner colleagues. Build required approvals into the workflow before commitments are made.

Resolve uncertainty early

Where conditions are unclear or appear to conflict, seek clarification through the authorised channel. Keep the written interpretation and any approval with the project records.

Keep the tracker current

Review obligations when the budget, activities, partners or agreement changes. A tracker supports implementation; it does not replace the source agreement.
