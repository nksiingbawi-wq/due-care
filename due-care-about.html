A personal introduction

Hello, I’m Siing.

I work in humanitarian risk and compliance, supporting teams to identify risks, strengthen internal controls and follow through on commitments.

My experience includes compliance reviews, partner due diligence, risk assessment and audit follow-up. An interest in teaching and research shapes how I work: ask clear questions, examine the evidence and explain ideas in ways people can use.

Due Care is my space for making compliance more understandable: how to check expenditure, assess partner capacity, prepare for audits and turn recommendations into practical improvements.
