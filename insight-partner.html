Partner due diligence · Practical explainer

Before the first disbursement.

A partner assessment should help establish whether funds can be managed responsibly and what support or safeguards are needed. Follow the applicable organisational and donor procedures.

Look beyond the manual

Discuss governance, delegated authority, budgeting, accounting, banking arrangements, procurement, asset management and reporting. Ask for a walkthrough of a transaction and examine evidence that the stated controls operate.

Consider capacity and context

Understand staffing, transaction volume and operating constraints. For a small team, assess whether independent review or other compensating controls can address limited segregation of duties.

Make the decision evidence based

Document gaps, existing strengths and the basis for any risk rating. Agree actions with owners, evidence requirements and deadlines. Identify which conditions must be resolved before release of funds under the applicable rules.

Keep assessment and support connected

Communicate significant unresolved weaknesses to the authorised decision-maker. Record the funding decision and any approved safeguards, and tailor monitoring and capacity support to the assessed risks.
