Reflections · Personal introduction

Why I started Due Care.

Humanitarian work carries a responsibility to use resources carefully, treat people fairly and remain accountable for our decisions.

Due care means giving our decisions the attention they deserve. I created this space to explore how we meet that responsibility in practice. It brings together my interest in risk, compliance, teaching and research, and my belief that useful knowledge should be understandable.

A place to ask questions

What makes a control useful? How can we discuss a finding fairly? What evidence do we need before making a recommendation? These are the kinds of questions I hope to explore here.

Learning with care

Due Care is a place to share practical explanations and resources, reflect on professional judgment and stay open to different perspectives. It is also a reminder to keep learning.
