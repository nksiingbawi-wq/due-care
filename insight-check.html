What does a compliance check look like?

A compliance check compares a defined activity or transaction with the requirements that apply to it. The scope and evidence depend on the context.

1. Define the question

Agree the period, process and transactions to review. Identify the relevant policy, agreement or donor requirement, using the applicable version.

2. Gather and test evidence

Review supporting records, trace the transaction through the process and discuss gaps with the people involved. Document the sample selection and its limitations.

3. Assess the finding fairly

Distinguish between what the evidence confirms and what remains unclear. Missing documentation does not, by itself, establish misconduct. Ask for clarification before concluding.

4. Agree useful action

Explain the requirement, observed condition and risk. Recommend a proportionate improvement, with an owner, deadline and evidence needed for closure.
